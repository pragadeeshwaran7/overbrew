# Overbrewed — Autonomous AI Agent Studio

A clean, modern commercial landing page for **Overbrewed** inspired by the visual design language of **slice.bank.in**.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetics (Inspired by slice.bank.in)

1. **Color Canvas & Atmospheric Depth**:
   - Deep midnight obsidian (`#090B0C`, `#120A1F`) with soft atmospheric violet / purple radial ambient lighting.
   - Accents of soft lavender (`#D8B4FE`), electric violet (`#A855F7`), and clean white pill capsules (`#F7F5FF`).

2. **Typography**:
   - **Bricolage Grotesque**: Bold, modern grotesque display typography for hero and section headers with tight tracking and editorial confidence.
   - **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Highly legible body typography.

3. **Floating Pill Navigation**:
   - Translucent glass navbar with soft pill-shaped capsule links (`What we do`, `How it works`, `Get Access →`).
   - Clean lowercase brand mark: `overbrewed`.

4. **Hero & 3D Floating Perspective Card Stack**:
   - High-impact headline: *"Autonomous agents. Built for what matters."*
   - Slice-style quick-access email pill capsule.
   - Floating perspective card stack showing live autonomous execution telemetry (`[FINANCE]`, `[LEGAL]`, `[DEFENSE]`, `[SWARM]`) with sub-38ms latency and zero data retention.

5. **Core Capability Pillars ("We Do Agents")**:
   - **Financial & Accounting Agents**: 3-way invoice matching, multi-entity GAAP reconciliation, rolling 13-week runway stress simulation, and GST tax preparation.
   - **Legal & Contract Intelligence Agents**: Clause-level risk extraction, detection of uncapped liabilities, contract redlining, and DPDP/GDPR regulatory compliance.
   - **Risk, Fraud & Security Agents**: Sub-50ms pre-auth webhook blocking, synthetic identity detection, collusion graph traversal, and automated SAR filing.
   - **Custom Enterprise Swarms**: Bespoke multi-agent autonomous swarms deployed on dedicated private Cloud VPC enclaves with zero data retention.

6. **How It Works**:
   - 3-step roadmap: `01 Define Your Workflow`, `02 We Build & Deploy`, `03 Autonomous 24/7 Run`.

7. **Early Access & Consultation**:
   - Slice-style minimalist capsule form to request an agent blueprint within 24 hours.
