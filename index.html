BUILT AT OVERBREWED

Meet FinTwin.

A smarter way to explore financial scenarios.

FinTwin is our financial intelligence product, designed to bring financial information, forecasting tools, scenario analysis, and AI-assisted explanations into one workspace. Instead of looking at financial figures in isolation, FinTwin aims to help users explore how changes in income, expenses, market conditions, and business assumptions could affect their financial outlook.

What FinTwin is designed to help with

Financial visibility

Bring relevant financial information together in one place.

Forecasting and scenario analysis

Explore possible financial outcomes by adjusting assumptions and testing different scenarios.

Anomaly detection

Use analytical models to help identify unusual patterns that may warrant further investigation.

AI-assisted insights

Ask questions about financial information and receive explanations grounded in the data available to the system.

HOW WE BUILD

Start with the problem. Engineer the solution.

We don't begin with an AI model looking for something to do. We begin with a problem worth solving.

01

Understand

We work with you to understand the workflow, the people involved, the data available, and the outcome that matters.

02

Design

We define the solution architecture, choose appropriate models and tools, and identify where human review or additional safeguards are necessary.

03

Build and Validate

We develop a focused prototype and test it against representative data. We measure performance against agreed requirements rather than relying on assumptions.

04

Deploy and Improve

Where appropriate, we integrate the solution into your existing workflow, establish monitoring, and improve it using observed performance and feedback.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Good AI is engineered, not just prompted.

We build practical systems with discipline, measurable benchmarks, and responsible human controls.

01

Purpose before complexity

We choose technology based on the problem, not the popularity of a model or framework.

02

Grounded in data
